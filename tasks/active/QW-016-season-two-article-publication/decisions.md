# QW-016 Decisions

1. **No "Part N" in the title.** The Wednesday slot has never used one and Parts VI and
   VII are already queued; numbering this piece would misorder the series.

2. **The length question is a decision to take, not a default to apply.** ~2,750 words
   against a 1,750-2,050 house length. Cutting a third out of a piece whose subject is
   a subtle two-claims-in-one-check bug risks removing the part that makes it land.
   Publishing long is allowed if chosen deliberately and recorded here.

3. **Publish after QW-015, not before.** Rejected alternative: publish now while the
   bug is live, which is a more honest snapshot but leaves the reader without the
   resolution and dates the piece the moment the fix lands. The cost of waiting is
   re-checking the quoted line numbers, which is cheap and is an acceptance criterion.

4. **The remote question is open.** Not yet decided whether `articles` gets a public
   remote, a private one, or a documented off-machine backup. What is decided is that
   "three finished drafts on one disk" is not an acceptable resting state.
